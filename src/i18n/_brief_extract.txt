1. How to read this brief
Each page is broken down into its sections in the order they appear on screen. For every section the brief gives the component type and the exact copy as it appears on the live site, so the page can be rebuilt or migrated like-for-like.

Meaning

Copy is transcribed verbatim, including punctuation and en/em dashes. Copy in quotation marks should be used exactly as written.
